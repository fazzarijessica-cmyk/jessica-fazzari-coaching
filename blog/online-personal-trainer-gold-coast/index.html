What online personal training includes

“Online coaching” covers everything from a downloadable template to a high-touch service with a real coach checking in every week. A good online program usually includes:

  • An individual training plan built around your goals, experience, schedule and equipment, rather than a generic template.
  • An app where your program lives, with exercise demonstrations and somewhere to log your workouts.
  • Regular check-ins so your coach can see how training is going and adjust the plan.
  • Form feedback, usually by sending a short video of a lift.
  • A way to ask questions between check-ins, such as messaging.

Red flags to watch for: programs that look the same for everyone, no check-ins at all, or a coach you never hear from after you pay. For a balanced look at when online coaching works and when it doesn't, read does online coaching work?

How online coaching works, week to week

Here's what a typical cycle looks like with Jess's online coaching:

Getting set up

You start with a short application (about five minutes), then a free 30-minute consult to talk through your goals and pick a package. Your personalised program is then delivered in the Everfit app, with exercise videos so you can see how each movement is done.

Training through the week

You train at your own gym, on your own schedule. Each session is laid out in the app, and you log your sets and weights as you go with Everfit's workout tracking. That record gives your coach real information to work with, rather than a guess at how the week went.

Checking in

On Online Coaching, you have fortnightly progress check-ins and a monthly program review. On Holistic Online Coaching, you get a weekly personalised video check-in instead, plus direct messaging during coaching hours.

Form checks and adjustments

On Holistic, you can film a lift and send it for video form analysis and feedback. Your program is adjusted on an ongoing basis as you progress, so it keeps moving forward rather than staying the same for months.

Nutrition and accountability

Holistic Online Coaching also includes meal and macro guidance and recipe ideas, along with accountability, goal setting and support. Personal trainers in Australia give general healthy-eating guidance rather than individualised diet plans, and people with medical conditions should see a dietitian for specific advice (training.gov.au).

What an online personal trainer costs on the Gold Coast

Higher-touch coaching with frequent check-ins and form feedback generally costs more than template programs.

Is online coaching right for you?

Online coaching tends to suit you if you:

  • have access to a gym or suitable equipment
  • are reasonably comfortable finding your way around the gym
  • want structure, accountability and a plan, but don't need someone beside you every session
  • have a busy or changing schedule, or live too far from your coach to train in person regularly

It may not be the best first step if you've never lifted before and feel unsure about technique, or you're coming back from an injury. In-person coaching gives hands-on cueing and help with equipment set-up, and many coaches see a combination of in-person and online training as a strong middle ground (MyPTHub).
