Personal trainer in Helensvale: two ways to train

Jessica Fazzari Coaching · 7 November 2026 · 5 min read

If you're looking for a personal trainer in Helensvale, you have two ways to train with Jess Fazzari: in person at World Gym Ashmore, about 13 km away and roughly 11–12 minutes' drive depending on traffic, or online from home or a gym near you. Online coaching starts at $49 a week, and Hybrid Coaching with a weekly 1:1 session is $110 a week.

Option 1: in-person personal training at Ashmore

Jess coaches clients 1:1 at World Gym Ashmore on Olsen Avenue. From Helensvale it's about 13 km by road, roughly 11–12 minutes' drive depending on traffic (Rome2Rio). That's a manageable trip if you're already heading south for work, or if you live on the Ashmore side of Helensvale.

In-person sessions are the right choice if you want:

  • someone beside you cueing your technique in real time
  • help setting up equipment you haven't used before
  • the motivation of having a coach in the room
  • a fixed appointment that keeps you accountable

With Jess, in-person training comes through Hybrid Coaching ($110 a week, which includes one 45-minute session each week at Ashmore plus full online coaching) or as a one-off 45-minute session for $79.

Option 2: online coaching from home or your local gym

Not everyone in Helensvale wants to drive to train. If you'd rather work out closer to home, whether that's a gym near Westfield Helensvale or your own garage set-up, online coaching brings the structure of personal training to wherever you train.

Here's what that looks like day to day:

  • Your personalised training program sits in the Everfit app, with exercise videos and workout tracking, so you can see each exercise demonstrated before you do it.
  • You log your sessions in the app, which gives your coach a clear picture of how your training is going.
  • You check in regularly (fortnightly on Online Coaching, weekly by personalised video on Holistic Online Coaching).
  • On Holistic, you can send videos of your lifts for form feedback and message during coaching hours.
  • Your program is reviewed and adjusted as you progress.

When you talk to any online coach, tell them what equipment you have access to, so your program fits where you'll actually be training.

Which option suits a Helensvale local?

There's no single right answer, but these rules of thumb help:

  • New to strength training? Some in-person coaching while you learn the main movements is usually worthwhile. Hybrid gives you that once a week without the cost of multiple sessions.
  • Comfortable in a gym already? Online coaching is generally cheaper and more flexible, and video form checks cover a lot of what you'd get in person.
  • Busy with kids, commuting or shift work? Online removes the need to be somewhere at a fixed time.
  • Somewhere in between? Start online and book a $79 session at Ashmore whenever you want your technique checked face to face.

Many coaches suggest a combination of in-person and online training as a strong middle ground (MyPTHub). You can read more in online coaching vs personal training.

Questions to ask any Helensvale personal trainer

Whether you train with Jess or someone else, it's worth asking a few questions before you commit. The Better Health Channel suggests checking a trainer's qualifications (including first aid), experience, references and insurance (public liability and professional indemnity). On top of that, ask:

  • Have you worked with people who have goals like mine?
  • What's included in the price, and what costs extra?
  • How will my program change as I get stronger?
  • How do we stay in touch between sessions?
  • What happens if my schedule changes?

A good trainer will be happy to answer all of these clearly, and you should come away knowing exactly what you're paying for each week.

Is online coaching effective?

It can be, but it depends on the program and on you. Online coaching tends to work best for people who are reasonably self-motivated and have access to a gym or suitable equipment. A good online program includes an individual plan rather than a generic template, regular check-ins, video form checks and a way to message your coach. Red flags include cookie-cutter programs and coaches you never hear from. For a balanced look, read does online coaching work?
