Online personal training for Burleigh Heads, Miami, Palm Beach and Coolangatta

The southern Gold Coast has a strong outdoor and active culture, and no shortage of places to train. What's often harder to find is structure: a plan that tells you what to do each session, progresses over time, and has someone checking in on it.

That's what online coaching provides. Your coach writes your program, you train where it suits you, and you stay in contact through an app. You don't need to be near your coach, and you're not tied to fixed appointment times. For people in the southern suburbs, that removes the biggest barrier to working with a coach based further north.

What to check before choosing an online coach

Not all online coaching is the same. Before you sign up with anyone, ask:

  • Is the program individual? It should be written around your goals, experience, schedule and equipment, not handed out to everyone.
  • How often will we check in? Regular check-ins are what turn a program into coaching.
  • Can I get feedback on my technique? Video form checks are one of the most useful parts of online coaching.
  • How do I ask questions? Find out whether you can message your coach between check-ins, and when.
  • Are they qualified? In Australia, the Certificate IV in Fitness is the standard qualification for personal trainers. Registration bodies such as AUSactive also look for current first aid and CPR.

Making online coaching work in the southern suburbs

Online coaching gives you flexibility, which means the structure has to come from your week. A few things help:

  • Choose a gym that's easy to get to. The closer it is to home or work, the fewer excuses there are to skip a session.
  • Set training days, not just a number of sessions. Australia's physical activity guidelines recommend muscle-strengthening activity on at least two days a week. Pick which days those are and protect them.
  • Use your check-ins. Be honest about missed sessions, sore spots and lifts that feel off. That's how your program gets better.
  • Film your main lifts. A quick video from your phone lets your coach check technique without being there.
