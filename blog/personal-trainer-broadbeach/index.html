Personal trainer for Broadbeach: Hybrid or fully online coaching

Jessica Fazzari Coaching · 7 November 2026 · 5 min read

If you live in Broadbeach and want a personal trainer, the two most practical options with Jess Fazzari are Hybrid Coaching ($110/week), which is online coaching through the week plus one 45-minute 1:1 session at World Gym Ashmore, or fully online coaching from $49/week at a gym near you. Ashmore is roughly 12–13 km from Broadbeach by road.

Why a hybrid approach makes sense in Broadbeach

Broadbeach has plenty going for it, but for many people the week is full: work, family, the beach, dinner plans around Pacific Fair. Committing to two or three in-person PT sessions a week can be hard to sustain when your trainer isn't around the corner.

The hybrid model solves that by splitting the job in two. Most of your training happens on your own, at a gym that suits you, following a program written for you. Then once a week you train 1:1 with your coach, who checks your technique, adjusts your weights and plans what comes next. Many coaches recommend a mix of online and in-person training for exactly this reason (MyPTHub).

What Hybrid Coaching includes

Jess's Hybrid Coaching is $110 a week and includes everything in her Holistic Online Coaching package, plus one in-person session:

  • a personalised training program in the Everfit app, with exercise videos and workout tracking
  • weekly personalised video check-ins
  • direct messaging support during coaching hours
  • video form analysis and feedback
  • ongoing program adjustments
  • meal and macro guidance and recipe ideas
  • accountability, goal setting and support
  • one 45-minute 1:1 PT session each week at World Gym Ashmore

If you want more time in person in a particular week, extra 45-minute sessions are $79 each.

What happens in your weekly in-person session

On Hybrid, the 45-minute session at Ashmore is where the hands-on coaching happens. It's the time to:

  • learn new exercises before they appear in your program
  • have your technique on your main lifts checked and cued in real time
  • work out the right weights to use for the coming week
  • talk through what went well, what felt hard, and what needs to change

The rest of the week, you train on your own with your program in the Everfit app, log your sessions, and keep in touch through check-ins and messages. That way the in-person time is used for the things that genuinely need a coach in the room. For more on balancing sessions, read how often you should see a personal trainer.

Getting to World Gym Ashmore from Broadbeach

World Gym Ashmore is on Olsen Avenue in Ashmore, roughly 12–13 km from Broadbeach by road. How long that takes depends heavily on the time of day and traffic, so it's worth doing a trial run at the time you'd actually be training.

Because Hybrid Coaching only needs one trip a week, the drive is far easier to fit in than multiple sessions. Many people pair it with something else they already do in the area, or pick a quieter time of day.

Is fully online coaching enough?

For some people in Broadbeach, yes. Online coaching tends to work best if you:

  • already feel comfortable in a gym and know your way around the equipment
  • are reasonably self-motivated and want structure more than supervision
  • have a gym you like near home or work
  • want the lowest weekly cost

It may not be the best starting point if you're completely new to strength training, returning from an injury, or unsure whether your technique is safe. In those cases, some in-person coaching early on is usually worthwhile. You can always start with Hybrid and move to online later, or start online and book an occasional $79 session to have your lifts checked. Read does online coaching work? for a balanced look.

How to decide

Ask yourself three questions:

  1. Can I get to Ashmore once a week, most weeks? If yes, Hybrid is worth considering. If not, start online.
  2. How confident am I with technique? Less confident points towards more in-person time; more confident points towards online.
  3. How much support do I want between sessions? If you want weekly check-ins, messaging and form feedback, Holistic or Hybrid include them. Online Coaching suits people who need less hands-on contact.

Australia's physical activity guidelines recommend muscle-strengthening activity on at least two days a week, so whichever option you choose, a program that covers the days you train alone is important.
